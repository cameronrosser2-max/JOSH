Tips for Hiring Commission Reps

  • Look for reps with existing relationships in your target market
  • Commission-only reps are self-motivated — they only earn when you earn
  • Set clear commission structure (% of sale, recurring, tiered) upfront
  • Provide strong sales materials — good reps need good tools
  • Consider a draw against commission to attract top talent initially
